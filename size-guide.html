Room Placement Guide

Tips for placing rugs in different rooms.

Living Room

Rug should be large enough to fit under the front legs of all furniture.

Bedroom

Rug should extend at least 60 cm beyond the sides and foot of the bed.

Dining Room

Rug should extend at least 60 cm beyond the table on all sides.

How to Measure

Follow these simple steps to get accurate measurements

Ring Size

  1. Wrap a strip of paper around your finger
  2. Mark where the paper overlaps
  3. Measure the length in millimeters
  4. Use the chart to find your ring size

Bracelet/Wrist

  1. Wrap a measuring tape around your wrist
  2. Take the measurement where you'd wear the bracelet
  3. Add 1-2 cm for a comfortable fit
  4. Use the chart to find your bracelet size

Clothing

  1. Measure chest: around the fullest part
  2. Measure waist: around the narrowest part
  3. Measure hips: around the fullest part
  4. Compare with our size chart

Rugs

  1. Measure the length and width of your space
  2. Consider furniture placement
  3. Leave 30-60 cm of floor space around the rug
  4. Choose the size that fits best
